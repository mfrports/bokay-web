Support

Something not right, or an idea? Tell us here. Every message is read and answered.

What does Bokay need?

A Mac with macOS 27 or later. Apple silicon makes everything faster, Remove most of all.

Which cameras' raw files can it open?

Every raw format your Mac can read, which is hundreds of cameras, plus JPEG, HEIC, PNG and TIFF. When Apple adds a camera to macOS, Bokay can open it too.

Where are my edits?

Your photos are never changed. Each photo's edit is kept in Bokay's own folder on your Mac, recognised by the photo itself, so it follows a photo that you rename or move. To see your work in other apps, export it: JPEG, HEIC, PNG or 16-bit TIFF.

How do I move to a new Mac?

Migration Assistant brings Bokay's folder along with everything else. To copy it by hand, quit Bokay and copy ~/Library/Containers/com.erik.Bokay to the same place on the new Mac.

Photos on a NAS

Click Add Photos… and choose your server from the list, or Connect to a server… for an address. Bokay reads each photo once and keeps a small print of it on your Mac, so your server's disks are spared, and it never writes anything there.

The first removal takes a few minutes

The very first time you remove something, macOS readies Bokay's model for your Mac, which can take a few minutes. It keeps it ready after that, and each removal takes a few seconds.

Using a cloud model for Remove

Remove works on your Mac, free and offline. If you would rather use OpenAI, Google Gemini or FLUX, choose it under Made by and paste an API key of your own. Only the square around what you remove is sent, straight to that provider, and billed to your account there.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
