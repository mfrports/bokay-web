Privacy

Bokay collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your photos and your edits stay on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

Bokay reads your photos where they are and never changes them. What you do to a photo (its edit, its rating) and the small prints Bokay shows on the light table are kept in Bokay's own folder on your Mac. Remove, Areas and Auto run on your Mac too: nothing about your photos is sent to us or to anyone else.

When Bokay goes online

Only in these cases, and only because you asked for it:

  • A cloud model for Remove, with your own key. If you choose OpenAI, Google Gemini or FLUX for Remove and give Bokay an API key of your own, Bokay sends that provider the square of the photo around what you remove, and your key, so the provider can draw the fill. It goes straight from your Mac to the provider under your own agreement with them. We never see it. Your key is kept in your Mac's keychain. Until you choose a cloud model, nothing is sent.
  • Photos on a server in your home or office. When you open the panel to add photos, Bokay looks for servers on your local network, such as a NAS, and connects to the ones you choose. Passwords you ask it to remember are kept in your keychain.
  • Raw files. macOS may download what it needs to read a newer camera's raw files from Apple.

Apple

You buy Bokay through the App Store, so Apple handles the purchase under Apple's privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage figures. We use them only to fix and improve Bokay.

Children

Bokay collects no data from anyone, children included.
